# Privacy Policy - Eastside Volleyball App

**Last Updated:** December 24, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Eastside Volleyball ("**we**," "**us**," "**our**," or "**Company**") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "**App**").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** Username, email address, password, first name, last name
- **Profile Information:** Player number, position, height, team role
- **Communication Data:** Messages, announcements, team communications
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, device ID
- **Location Data:** Approximate location (only if you grant permission)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** Pages viewed, time spent, features used, error logs
- **Device Data:** IP address, browser type, operating system version
- **Analytics:** App crashes, performance metrics

### 2.3 Third-Party Information

- **Video Content:** Metadata from uploaded game videos (title, date, duration)
- **Team Data:** Player statistics, announcements from coaches

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for:

1. **Account Management**
   - Creating and maintaining your account
   - Authentication and security
   - Password recovery

2. **Service Delivery**
   - Providing app features (profiles, stats, videos, announcements)
   - Personalized player dashboard
   - Team communications

3. **Analytics & Improvement**
   - Understanding how users interact with the app
   - Fixing bugs and improving performance
   - Developing new features

4. **Communications**
   - Sending important updates about the app
   - Responding to support requests
   - Team announcements

5. **Legal Compliance**
   - Complying with applicable laws
   - Enforcing terms of service
   - Protecting against fraud

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

### 4.1 We Share Information With

- **Team Members:** Your profile and stats are visible to other team members
- **Coaches:** Coaches can see all player information for management purposes
- **Service Providers:** Third-party services that help us operate the app

### 4.2 We DO NOT Share

- Personal information is never sold to third parties
- We do not share email addresses with marketers
- Financial information is not shared without explicit consent

---

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures

- SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
- Secure authentication using tokens
- Password encryption using industry-standard algorithms
- Regular security audits

---

## 6. Your Rights & Choices

### 6.1 Access & Control

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request corrections to inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Export your data

---

## 7. Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** Data is retained while your account is active
- **After Deletion:** Data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion
